# Reinforcement learning: the dialogue policy against hand-written policies

Generated by `ml/rl/evaluate_rl.py` from the shipped policy (`studyroute-dm-v1`); do not edit by hand. Every number is on the **test goals of the simulator**, which were never used to train or to choose a run. The simulator is an assumption, not a user study: see [AI_RL.md](AI_RL.md) for what it assumes and what that means.

6,000 simulated conversations per policy (1,200 test goals, five parent personalities each). Return = the reward the policy was trained on: +1 for a correct plan, +0.5 for a correct deadline or period, -2 for a wrong amount, -1 for a wrong deadline or period, -0.05 for every question asked, -0.5 if the parent gives up. 95% bootstrap intervals.

## How to read this honestly

* The parents are simulated, and the rewards are my choices. A policy trained on them is only as good as that assumption; the sensitivity table shows how much the behaviour depends on it.
* What the policy perceives is real: every sentence went through the assistant's own reader and checks, so the noise (missing values, values read two ways, a second amount) is the real system's.
* The policy cannot see whether a value is correct. It sees who read it, how sure the model was, whether the message mentioned another amount, and the turn count.
* None of its actions moves money. The worst outcome is an extra question or a wrong quote card that the person reviews before creating a draft, and a human still approves.
